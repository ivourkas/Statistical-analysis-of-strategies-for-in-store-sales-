# Statistical Analysis of Strategies for In-Store Sales  


This project was implemented as part of the graduate course **Applied Statistics** in the **Data Science and Engineering** program at CCNY. The course was selected during my Ph.D. studies in Electrical Engineering at the City College of New York.  

The aim of the project is to simulate and statistically analyze three different selling strategies for a medical supply store. Using Monte Carlo simulation, we identify the best strategies based on profit maximization and optimization of storage space.
